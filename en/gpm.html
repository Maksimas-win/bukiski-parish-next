The basics in a minute

GPM (gyventojų pajamų mokestis) is Lithuanian personal income tax. The law lets you ask VMI to transfer part of the tax you have already paid to a recipient of your choice. It is not a new payment and not a further deduction from your salary.

  • Not a share of your salary

    The share is calculated from the year’s GPM, not from income. Example: €1,000 GPM × 0.012 = €12.

  • Nothing extra to pay

    The money has already been paid to the state. You only decide where up to 1.2% of it goes. Without an application the whole amount stays in the budget.

  • Apply only in EDS

    This section helps you prepare. Form FR0512 is completed in the official VMI declaration system, not on the parish website.

  • Keep the years apart

    The 2027 campaign concerns tax on income received in 2026. Filing period: January 1, 2027 — May 3, 2027.

Who this is for

Permanent residents of Lithuania for tax purposes (nuolatiniai Lietuvos gyventojai) with income subject to GPM in 2026: salary, self-employment or other taxable income. Citizenship alone neither grants nor removes the right. A pensioner or someone temporarily out of work can take part if GPM was calculated or withheld on their income during the year; if the tax is zero, there is nothing to allocate. You can check your own situation with VMI.

How it differs from a donation

GPM share
Part of tax already paid. Transferred by VMI, usually between July 1 and November 15. Filed as FR0512 in EDS. Your wallet is unchanged.
Bank donation
Your own money, transferred to the parish account at any time. The details are in a separate block below. One way does not exclude the other.

How much is that in money?

Enter your income tax (GPM) for 2026. This is not your salary: the tax amount appears in the annual GPM311 return in EDS, in your employer’s statement or in Mano VMI. The calculation runs in your browser and is not sent anywhere.

All calculations are performed locally. The values you enter are neither stored nor transmitted.

Before submitting, check

  • The recipient code 301004570 and the name Bukiškio stačiatikių Kristaus Gimimo parapija match what EDS shows.
  • The share is no more than 1.2% (in total for all recipients in this category).
  • The first tax period is 2026; the last is no later than 2030.
  • If you must file the annual GPM311 return, it has been filed on time — otherwise the support is not transferred.
  • After submitting, the application status is visible in EDS: Pateikti dokumentai and Pranešimai. VMI transfers the money later, usually between July 1 and November 15.

The checklist is for self-checking. The parish website cannot see your application or confirm that it was received.

If you have filed an application before

A multi-year application usually remains in force, but it is worth checking — especially if the recipient was unavailable last year.

  1. 1

    Check that it is still valid

    In EDS open your filed documents (Pateikti dokumentai) and find the latest FR0512: which years and which recipient it names. If the period covers 2026 and the recipient is correct, no new application is needed.

  2. 2

    If the recipient was not on the list

    Religious communities regain the right to support from 2027. If your earlier application named another recipient or was rejected, file a new application for 2026 during the filing period.

  3. 3

    If you need to change it

    A new application for the same period replaces the previous one. An application already filed for 2026 can be corrected in EDS until June 30, 2027: change the recipient, share or period. This is a correction deadline, not an extension for filing.

  4. 4

    Where to see the result

    In EDS notifications (Pranešimai) and in Mano VMI. Acceptance does not yet mean a transfer: VMI usually transfers support between July 1 and November 15.

Answers to your questions

Short answers to real difficulties. The search runs in your browser over the content of this page.

No exact answer. See all the questions below or contact the parish.

Do I have to pay anything extra?

No. FR0512 allocates part of the income tax you have already paid. Nothing is debited from your account and your salary is not reduced. A bank transfer is a separate, voluntary way to help.

Explain it simply

What is the 1.2% calculated from?

From the annual GPM for 2026 according to the return (or the tax withheld by the employer if no return is required), not from salary. VMI determines the exact base. The calculator only multiplies the amount entered by 0.012.

FR0512 helper

What if I don’t know my tax amount?

Look at the annual GPM311 return for 2026 in EDS or the data in Mano VMI; your employer issues a statement of tax withheld. To get a sense of scale, the calculator has an “I don’t know my GPM amount” mode with teaching examples — it is not an estimate of your tax.

FR0512 helper

Does this apply to someone in my situation?

If you are a permanent resident of Lithuania for tax purposes and GPM was calculated or withheld on your income in 2026 — yes, regardless of citizenship or age. If there was no taxable income and the tax is zero, there is nothing to allocate. If in doubt, check with VMI.

Explain it simply

Can I choose several recipients?

Yes. Shares for recipients in this category must total no more than 1.2%. Separately and in addition you can allocate up to 0.6% to political organisations and up to 0.6% to trade unions. An amount below €3 to one recipient for a year is not transferred.

Open the helper

Can I allocate support for several years?

Yes. FR0512 includes a period: for the 2027 campaign the first tax year is 2026 and the last is no later than 2030. Such an application applies to all the years named, as long as the recipient remains eligible.

Open the helper

Do I need to file every year?

No, if a valid application covers the relevant year and the right recipient. If you named only one year, file again in the next campaign. A new application for the same period replaces the previous one.

See what to do

How do I check an application I filed earlier?

In EDS open Pateikti dokumentai, find the latest FR0512 and look at the recipient, share and years. An application for 2026 can be corrected until June 30, 2027. This website cannot see your application.

See what to do

What if the recipient cannot be found?

Check that you are entering the code 301004570 without spaces and compare the name Bukiškio stačiatikių Kristaus Gimimo parapija. Do not choose a similar name of another organisation. VMI publishes recipient data; if the parish is not on the list, tell us and we will find out why.

FR0512 helper

How do I know the application was accepted?

Only in EDS: in the document status and in notifications (Pranešimai). Acceptance does not yet mean a transfer — VMI usually transfers support between July 1 and November 15. If you must file GPM311 and did not file it on time, the support is not transferred.

Open the helper

How does GPM differ from a bank transfer?

The GPM share is part of tax already paid, transferred by VMI on your application. A bank transfer is your own money sent directly to the parish at any time. You can do both.

FR0512 helper

Who can help me work it out?

The parish — by phone or messenger: we will explain the steps, but we will not log in to your bank or EDS on your behalf and will not ask for passwords. Official explanations and advice come from VMI: the pages in the sources section and the VMI helpline.
