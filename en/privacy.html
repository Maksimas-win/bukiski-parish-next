Privacy

This website uses no analytics, advertising pixels or embedded maps. Its main content is available without registration.

Reading settings

Appearance, text size and contrast preferences are saved in your browser’s localStorage. The reset button removes them. The website sets no cookies.

Prayer requests

When you press “Send”, the names, your name, email and note are passed to the parish through the Formspree service (Formspree, Inc., USA) and arrive in the parish mailbox. Until then the names stay only in this tab and are erased when you leave the page. The website itself does not store requests. Printouts and downloaded files stay with you.

External links

Map, calendar, transport, social media, WhatsApp and banking services process data under their own rules. You reach them only by clicking.

Technical data

The hosting provider may receive your IP address, request time and browser information to deliver pages and protect the service. The parish must identify the provider and retention periods before public launch.

Data enquiries

The intended controller is Bukiškio stačiatikių Kristaus Gimimo parapija. Before launch, the parish must confirm the controller, legal bases and contact channel. You can currently contact the parish on +370 636 93506.
